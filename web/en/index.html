Questions

Short answers.

Is this tax advice?

No. Deklara compiles your records into a clean, filing-ready document. For individual tax questions, consult a tax advisor or your cantonal tax office.

Do I have to pay tax on capital gains?

For private investors, price gains are generally tax-free in Switzerland. What must be declared: your holdings as of 31 Dec (wealth tax) and income from staking, lending or mining (income tax).

Which cantons are supported?

All 26. Your securities register is generated in your canton's format — from Zurich to Geneva.

What happens to my data?

Your records are used exclusively to create your document and can be fully deleted after export. Swiss hosting is planned.

My transaction history is incomplete. Does it still work?

Yes — that's exactly what Deklara is built for. You don't need a complete transaction history, just evidence of your holdings as of 31 Dec and your income.

I never declared previous years. Can you help?

That's planned: Deklara can also compile several past tax years as the basis for a penalty-free voluntary disclosure (Selbstanzeige). Just tick the option when signing up — you'll be the first to know when it's available.
